THE GUARDIAN X WORKFLOW

Six stages. One clear response.

Guardian X separates the safety process into distinct stages. Each stage contributes information to the next, allowing Guardian AI to produce a response based on the complete assessment rather than a single piece of data.

01 Location Checking

Verifies and identifies the requested destination.

02 Security Data

Collects available safety-related information.

03 Location Comparison

Compares the destination with relevant location information.

04 Risk Analysis

Analyzes identified conditions and potential concerns.

05 Safety Assessment

Builds an overall assessment from the processed information.

06 AI Response

Turns the assessment into understandable guidance and suggestions.
